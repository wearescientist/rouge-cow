# 《深根之疫》开场动画 - 10秒白色菌丝版

> Logo固定左上角，**上下都留白**

---

## 【分镜 1】归家惊变（3秒）
**运镜**: 牛牛从左侧跑入，镜头跟随 reveal 家园

**画面**: 牛牛跑向家园，镜头上摇 reveal——帐篷树木全被**白色菌丝**包裹成茧，菌丝蠕动发光。牛牛震惊僵住。

**豆包提示词**:
```
Pixel art animation, small white cow running from left toward home,
camera pans up to reveal tent and trees completely covered in thick white mycelium,
white fungal silk writhing with bioluminescent glow,
cow freezes in shock,
game logo "深根之疫" top left pixel font,
16-bit style, 1920x1080, top 150px black margin, bottom 150px black margin
```

---

## 【分镜 2】骨片警示（2秒）
**运镜**: 急速下移到地面特写

**画面**: 白色菌丝丛中，一片发光骨片。金色文字"**别下来**"，旁边有指向洞穴的拖痕。

**豆包提示词**:
```
Pixel art, extreme close up on ground covered in white mycelium,
glowing bone fragment with golden text "别下来",
drag marks leading to cave entrance,
contrast between white fungus and golden light,
logo top left, 16-bit style, top 150px black margin, bottom 150px black margin
```

---

## 【分镜 3】歌声召唤（2秒）
**运镜**: 从骨片拉出到洞穴

**画面**: 镜头后拉 reveal 洞穴入口，蓝绿色脉动光芒，蓝色音符符号从洞中飘出（母亲歌声）。白色菌丝一直延伸到洞口。

**豆包提示词**:
```
Pixel art, camera pulls back from bone to dark cave entrance,
blue green pulsing light deep inside cave,
blue musical note symbols floating out representing mother song,
white mycelium covering ground leading to cave,
logo top left, 16-bit style, top 150px black margin, bottom 150px black margin
```

---

## 【分镜 4】踏入深渊（3秒）
**运镜**: 跟随牛牛背影走入洞穴

**画面**: 牛牛站在白色菌丝中，面对洞穴。眼神从犹豫变坚定，迈步走入黑暗。身影被蓝绿光芒吞没。

**豆包提示词**:
```
Pixel art, small white cow standing on white fungal covered ground,
facing massive dark cave entrance,
expression changes from hesitation to determination,
walks into cave swallowed by darkness and blue glow,
logo top left, 16-bit style, top 150px black margin, bottom 150px black margin
```
